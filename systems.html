/ Infrastructure Categories

Five operating environments. One architecture.

Autonomous infrastructure is the physical and digital operating environment that lets vehicles and distributed assets work with reduced local human intervention: storing, protecting, launching, recovering, powering, servicing, and coordinating them.

Illustrative Configuration
/ 01 · Air Infrastructure

Recover, protect, recharge, relaunch.

Robotic hangars and landing platforms where an aircraft lands, is centered, charged or given a fresh battery, has its payload exchanged, and is protected from weather until its next sortie.

  • Robotic UAV Hangars
  • Landing Platforms
  • Battery Exchange
  • Payload Exchange
  • Remote Operating Stations
Illustrative Configuration · USV capture
/ 02 · Maritime Infrastructure

Docking and capture, built for salt water.

Robotic USV hangars and pier-integrated docks with mechanical capture, shore power, payload handling, and corrosion-resistant structure, so a vessel can arrive, be secured, and be serviced without a crew on the pier.

  • Robotic USV Hangars
  • Docking Stations
  • Recovery Systems
  • Charging + Fueling
  • Payload Handling
Illustrative Configuration
/ 03 · Land Infrastructure

Garages and shelters for ground robots.

UGV garages and robotic shelters where a ground vehicle aligns, connects to automated charging, exchanges batteries, tools, or payloads, and is inspected before it returns to work.

  • UGV Garages
  • Robotic Shelters
  • Charging + Refueling
  • Tool Exchange
  • Maintenance Cells
Illustrative Configuration
/ 04 · Space-Support Infrastructure

Ground systems that handle flight hardware.

Payload processing, robotic handling, integration fixtures, clean enclosures, transportation, and test support: the ground infrastructure that moves spacecraft hardware safely from integration to the launch site.

  • Payload Processing
  • Robotic Handling
  • Integration Fixtures
  • Clean Enclosures
  • Ground Support Equipment
Illustrative Configuration
/ 05 · Distributed Mission Infrastructure

Operating capacity, wherever the mission is.

Networked, field-deployable nodes combining communications relays, energy systems, remote sensing, fleet support, and remote operations, so distributed assets stay supported far from a fixed base.

  • Fleet Support Nodes
  • Communications Relays
  • Energy Systems
  • Remote Sensor Stations
  • Mobile Command
/ Product Families

What Asgard is building.

Eleven families of autonomous infrastructure, developed on one common system architecture and executed through one industrial model. These are design concepts in development; none is presented as a fielded product.

Illustrative Configuration
01 / Product Family

UAV Hangar Platform

Robotic infrastructure for aircraft protection, recovery, charging, payload handling, inspection, data transfer, and mission turnaround.

Illustrative Configuration
02 / Product Family

USV Hangar Platform

Autonomous maritime infrastructure for docking, protection, charging or fueling, payload operations, inspection, maintenance support, and redeployment.

Illustrative Configuration
03 / Product Family

UGV Support Station

Infrastructure for robotic ground systems including charging, battery exchange, tool exchange, payload exchange, inspection, maintenance, and fleet support.

Illustrative Configuration
04 / Product Family

Mobile Autonomous Support Node

Deployable infrastructure supporting autonomous assets in expeditionary, remote, or distributed operating environments.

Illustrative Configuration
05 / Product Family

Launch and Recovery Platform

Integrated equipment for controlled vehicle deployment, positioning, capture, recovery, and servicing.

Modules + platforms · 06 to 11

06 / Product Family

Distributed Charging Platform

Conductive or inductive charging with automated connector engagement, battery health and thermal monitoring, and charge scheduling for mixed fleets.

07 / Product Family

Robotic Payload Exchange Platform

Robotic removal and installation of sensor and mission payloads with mechanical locking, electrical and data connection, functional verification, and configuration control.

08 / Product Family

Robotic Tool Exchange Platform

Tool identification, storage, and positioning for robotic pickup and release, with utility connection, tool verification, and calibration status tracking.

09 / Product Family

Autonomous Maintenance Cell

Inspection, diagnostics, cleaning, module and sensor replacement, and minor repair, with each maintenance action traceable to the asset it was performed on.

10 / Product Family

Mission Command Shelter

Ruggedized shelters for remote operators to supervise fleets, monitor readiness, and coordinate distributed autonomous assets from the field.

11 / Product Family

Expeditionary Infrastructure Platform

Trailer-mounted and containerized infrastructure that deploys, levels, powers up, and establishes communications at a new site, then packs down to move.
