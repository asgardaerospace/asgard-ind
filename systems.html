/ Autonomous Infrastructure

Systems that keep autonomous assets in operation.

Physical and digital systems that keep autonomous assets available, supportable, and mission-ready — recovery, protection, power, payload handling, inspection, and redeployment.

Scroll
/ Why Infrastructure

An autonomous vehicle is not an autonomous operation.

Autonomous vehicles can navigate, sense, communicate, and execute missions independently. Their support environments often remain manual. Asgard closes that gap with robotic, physical, and digital infrastructure.

An Autonomous Vehicle Alone

Autonomy stops at the vehicle.

  • Manual recovery
  • Manual charging & fueling
  • Manual inspection
  • Manual payload exchange
  • Manual maintenance
  • Manual redeployment
An Autonomous Operational System

Autonomy extends to the operation.

  • Robotic recovery & protection
  • Automated charging & servicing
  • Automated inspection
  • Payload & battery exchange
  • Digital coordination & data transfer
  • Remote, persistent operations
/ System Families

What Asgard builds.

Six families of autonomous infrastructure, engineered on one architecture and executed through one industrial model.

Product Family

UAV Hangar Platform

Robotic infrastructure for aircraft protection, recovery, charging, payload handling, inspection, data transfer, and mission turnaround.

Product Family

USV Hangar Platform

Autonomous maritime infrastructure for docking, protection, charging or fueling, payload operations, inspection, maintenance support, and redeployment.

Product Family

UGV Support Station

Infrastructure for robotic ground systems including charging, battery exchange, tool exchange, payload exchange, inspection, maintenance, and fleet support.

Product Family

Mobile Autonomous Support Node

Deployable infrastructure supporting autonomous assets in expeditionary, remote, or distributed operating environments.

Product Family

Launch & Recovery Systems

Integrated equipment for controlled vehicle deployment, positioning, capture, recovery, and servicing.

Product Family

Mission Support Systems

Ground support equipment, robotic handling, test systems, power systems, communications systems, and specialized mission infrastructure.
