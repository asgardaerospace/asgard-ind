/ 03 — Autonomous Infrastructure

Systems that keep autonomous assets in operation.

Physical and digital systems that keep autonomous assets available, supportable, and mission-ready: recovery, protection, power, payload handling, inspection, and redeployment.

Scroll
/ Why Infrastructure

It takes more than a vehicle to make an autonomous operation.

A vehicle alone — autonomy stops at the vehicle

  • Manual recovery
  • Manual charging & fueling
  • Manual inspection
  • Manual payload exchange
  • Manual maintenance
  • Manual redeployment

With Asgard infrastructure — autonomy extends to the operation

  • Robotic recovery & protection
  • Automated charging & servicing
  • Automated inspection
  • Payload & battery exchange
  • Digital coordination & data transfer
  • Remote, persistent operations
/ System Families

What Asgard is building.

Six families of autonomous infrastructure, developed on one architecture and executed through one industrial model. These are design concepts in development; none is presented as a fielded product.

Illustrative Configuration
01 / Product Family

UAV Hangar Platform
