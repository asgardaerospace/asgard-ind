Overview

Asgard Industries ("Asgard," "we," "us") operates asgardaerospace.com. This policy describes how we handle information when you visit the site or contact us.

The site does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information through forms. If you contact us by email at contact@asgardaerospace.com, we receive the information you choose to include — such as your name, email address, company, and the content of your message.

How we use information

Information you send us is used to respond to your inquiry, evaluate potential build or partnership engagements, and maintain a record of our correspondence. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

Data retention & your choices

We retain correspondence for as long as needed for the purposes above. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us by emailing contact@asgardaerospace.com.
