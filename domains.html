/ 02 — Sectors

One Asgard architecture. Five mission sectors.

Air, maritime, land, robotics, and space are mission applications of one Asgard architecture. The same engineering, Launchbelt orchestration, and Forge execution model stands behind every sector; only the mission environment changes.

01 / AirAlgiz · Ward

Airframes and uncrewed systems.

Stealth airframes, UAS structures, and avionics-ready assemblies, built from composite, thermoplastic, and machined metallic parts, qualified for flight from the first article.

  • Composite and thermoplastic airframe structures
  • Machined spars, ribs, and precision fittings
  • UAS assemblies integrated and tested to spec
  • Composite Layup
  • 5-Axis Machining
  • Assembly & Test
Sector / Air
02 / SeaLaguz · Water

Naval craft and autonomous vessels.

Hull structures, machined housings, and sensor-ready assemblies for naval platforms and autonomous surface and subsurface vessels, built to marine and defense quality standards.

  • Structural fabrication and welded assemblies
  • Corrosion-resistant machined components
  • Autonomy and sensing hardware integration
  • Fabrication
  • Welding
  • NDT & Test
Sector / Sea
03 / LandUruz · Strength

Ground platforms and tactical vehicles.

Structures, armor-ready fabrications, and precision drivetrain and chassis components for ground vehicles and expeditionary systems, produced at prototype pace and scaled to rate.

  • Heavy fabrication and machined structures
  • Vehicle subsystems and mounting hardware
  • Field-support and expeditionary equipment
  • Sheet Metal
  • Machining
  • Surface Treatment
Sector / Land
04 / SpaceSowilo · Sun

Satellites and orbital hardware.

Bus structures, precision optical mounts, and flight hardware for satellites and persistent orbital infrastructure, built with the tolerances and traceability space programs demand.

  • Precision-machined bus and payload structures
  • Thermal and optical mounting hardware
  • Full material genealogy for flight certification
  • Precision Machining
  • Additive
  • Cleanroom Assembly
Sector / Space
05 / RoboticsTiwaz · Victory

Robotics and automation.

Robotic systems, automated production cells, and precision motion hardware — for programs that field robots and for factories that run on them. Robotics is its own sector at Asgard, with dedicated engineering behind it.

  • Robotic arms, end effectors, and motion assemblies
  • Automated work cells and production tooling
  • Precision gears, actuators, and sensor mounts
  • Precision Motion
  • Automation Cells
  • Integration & Test
