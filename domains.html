One Architecture · Five Environments
/ 02 — Sectors

One Asgard architecture. Five mission sectors.

Air, maritime, land, robotics, and space are mission applications of one Asgard architecture. The same engineering, Launchbelt orchestration, and Forge execution model stands behind every sector; only the mission environment changes.

Scroll
01 / AirAlgiz · Ward

Air: airframes and uncrewed systems.

Asgard Industries is developing systems engineering, industrial execution, and autonomous infrastructure capability for atmospheric systems: uncrewed aircraft structures, airframe assemblies, and the ground infrastructure that recovers, services, and redeploys them. Target applications include composite, thermoplastic, and machined metallic airframe structures.

  • Composite and thermoplastic airframe structures
  • Machined spars, ribs, and precision fittings
  • UAS assemblies integrated and tested to spec
  • Composite Layup
  • 5-Axis Machining
  • Assembly & Test
Sector / Air
02 / SeaLaguz · Water

Sea: naval craft and autonomous vessels.

Asgard Industries is developing integrated support systems for autonomous maritime operations, including docking, launch and recovery, vehicle protection, charging or fuelling, and lifecycle support. Target applications include hull structures, machined housings, and sensor-ready assemblies for naval platforms and autonomous surface and subsurface vessels.

  • Structural fabrication and welded assemblies
  • Corrosion-resistant machined components
  • Autonomy and sensing hardware integration
  • Fabrication
  • Welding
  • NDT & Test
Sector / Sea
03 / LandUruz · Strength

Land: ground platforms and tactical vehicles.

Asgard Industries is developing systems for uncrewed ground vehicles, robotic platforms, and the infrastructure required to support autonomous ground operations. Target applications include structures, armour-ready fabrications, and precision drivetrain and chassis components for ground vehicles and expeditionary systems.

  • Heavy fabrication and machined structures
  • Vehicle subsystems and mounting hardware
  • Field-support and expeditionary equipment
  • Sheet Metal
  • Machining
  • Surface Treatment
Sector / Land
04 / SpaceSowilo · Sun

Space: satellites and orbital hardware.

Asgard Industries is building capability applicable to space hardware, ground support equipment, integration systems, handling, and test infrastructure. Target applications include bus structures, precision optical mounts, and hardware for satellites and orbital infrastructure, at the tolerances and traceability space programs require.

  • Precision-machined bus and payload structures
  • Thermal and optical mounting hardware
  • Full material genealogy for flight certification
  • Precision Machining
  • Additive
  • Cleanroom Assembly
Sector / Space
05 / RoboticsTiwaz · Victory

Robotics: robotic systems and automation.

Asgard Industries is developing robotic systems, automated production cells, and precision motion hardware, both for programs that field robots and for the factories that run on them. Robotics is treated as its own sector in the Asgard architecture rather than as a subset of another domain.

  • Robotic arms, end effectors, and motion assemblies
  • Automated work cells and production tooling
  • Precision gears, actuators, and sensor mounts
  • Precision Motion
  • Automation Cells
  • Integration & Test
Sector / Robotics
