/ Who We Partner With

Built for serious manufacturers.

We partner with shops that take quality and capability seriously, and we help good ones become great ones. Bring the capabilities you have today, and we'll help you build the rest.

/ Baseline to qualify

What we look for

  • ✓
    A documented quality system:ISO 9001 in place, or AS9100 for aerospace and defense work.
  • ✓
    Core process capability:machining, fabrication, composites, thermoplastics, assembly, or test.
  • ✓
    Domestic or allied production:supporting a resilient, trusted industrial base.
  • ✓
    ITAR registration:in place or willingness to register for controlled work.
  • ✓
    Available capacity:open hours, cells, or lines you're ready to put to work.
/ Where we help you grow

What we help you build

  • ✦
    Advanced process support:engineering support on the processes and documentation that higher-value programs ask for.
  • ✦
    Advanced materials:ramp into composites and thermoplastics with engineering support.
  • ✦
    Automation & throughput:process improvement to move from prototype into rate production.
  • ✦

  • ✦
    A deeper backlog:a track record on-network that unlocks larger, higher-value programs.
