/ The Thread

Eight states. One continuous thread.

Most programs lose continuity at the handoffs: between engineering and manufacturing, between suppliers and integrators, between delivery and sustainment. Launchbelt runs horizontally through the lifecycle and connects every state transition.

Required→ Designed→ Released→ Built→ Inspected→ Tested→ Deployed→ Maintained
Define

Requirements & Architecture

Requirements, system architecture, interfaces, and configuration: the controlled technical definition every downstream activity traces to.

Decompose

Work Packages & Suppliers

Released engineering decomposed into coordinated work packages, routed to qualified suppliers and Asgard capabilities.

Execute

Manufacturing & Quality

Manufacturing activity, quality evidence, and inspection records captured against the released definition as the work happens.

Verification and acceptance evidence linked back to the requirements they close, green to delivery.

Asset history, deployed configuration, and sustainment records maintained through the operational life of the system.
