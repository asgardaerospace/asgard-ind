/ The Thread

Eight states. One continuous thread.

Most programs lose continuity at the handoffs: between engineering and manufacturing, between suppliers and integrators, between delivery and sustainment. Launchbelt runs horizontally through the lifecycle and connects every state transition.

/ One System of Record

Every program, visible in one live thread.

Launchbelt is the software layer beneath the Asgard operating model. It scopes builds, attaches compliance requirements, matches capacity, and tracks every operation from raw material to delivered configuration — visible to program teams and suppliers in one place.
