/ Built for Early Stage to Scale

Ship hardware before you build a plant.

Standing up your own production line is the most expensive way to learn whether a design works. The Asgard model is designed to let early-stage companies reach qualified units through existing manufacturing capability, then scale as the program grows.

Path A · Build It Yourself

Stand up your own line

  • 01
    Heavy capex up front. Raise for tooling, machines, floor space, and certification before the first part ships.
  • 02
    Months to qualify. Build a quality system and chase certifications from scratch.
  • 03
    Fixed capacity. Pay for the line regardless of demand.
  • 04
    Slow to iterate. Every design change ripples through tooling you already paid for.
